# Thumbnails & YouTube SEO

## Thumbnail (design_thumbnail)
- 3–5 words max, huge, high contrast. The text should *complement* the title, not repeat it (title: "I Tried Sleeping 4 Hours a Day for 30 Days" → thumbnail text: "*BIG* MISTAKE").
- One focal point: a face with clear emotion, a striking object, or a big number. Use a real frame from the footage (`videoAssetId` + `time`) or a strong image.
- Templates: `bold-text` (text left, subject right), `face-reaction` (face cut-out with outline), `split` (A vs B), `minimal` (just bold words), `news` (headline over image), `number` (huge stat).
- Use the accent color on the one word that matters (`*word*`). Add an `emoji` or `arrow` only if it adds meaning.
- Check it at small size: if it doesn't read at 320px wide, simplify.

## Title
- ≤ 60 characters, keyword early, specific and curiosity-driven, honest (no clickbait that the video doesn't pay off).
- Offer 3–5 `titleOptions` with different angles (outcome, curiosity, how-to, list, contrarian).

## Description
1. First 2 lines: the hook + the main keyword (shown in search).
2. 2–4 sentence summary of what the viewer learns.
3. Chapters: `00:00 Intro` first, at least 3 chapters, each ≥ 10s, in order (YouTube makes them clickable).
4. Sources/links (cite research), credits for stock media and music.
5. 3 relevant hashtags at the end.

## Tags & chapters
- 8–15 tags: main keyword, variations, related topics. Don't stuff.
- Chapters come from the section boundaries of the timeline (script sections, chapter cards).
- Category id: 27 Education, 28 Science & Technology, 22 People & Blogs, 24 Entertainment, 26 Howto & Style, 25 News & Politics, 20 Gaming, 17 Sports, 10 Music.
